<h1 id="rulebook">Rulebook</h1>
<h2 id="contents--terminology">Contents &amp; Terminology</h2>
<p>Before diving in, here are a few terms used throughout this rulebook:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Asset</strong> — Anything on the board: buildings, creatures, and infrastructure tokens.</li>
<li><strong>Marker</strong> — The physical piece placed on the board to represent an asset.</li>
<li><strong>Counter</strong> — Small pieces placed on cards to track health, status effects, and elemental power.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-board">The Board</h2>
<p>The board is a <strong>15×10 grid</strong>. At the start of the game it is divided evenly, giving each player five rows of territory.</p>
<h2 id="setup">Setup</h2>
<ol>
<li>Divide the board so each player controls five rows.</li>
<li>Deal <strong>six cards</strong> to each player.</li>
<li>Both players begin on the <strong>Main Phase</strong> of their first turn, since no buildings have been constructed yet.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="turn-structure">Turn Structure</h2>
<p>Each turn consists of four phases, always performed in this order:</p>
<h3 id="generation">1. Generation</h3>
<p>You may choose <strong>one building</strong> to generate power. Place a power counter on a creature within that building’s range that matches its elemental type.</p>
<h3 id="passive-actions">2. Passive Actions</h3>
<p>Resolve the following in order:</p>
<ol>
<li>Each building that was <strong>not</strong> used to generate power performs its <strong>passive ability</strong>. Any order is acceptable.</li>
<li>Any creature that is <strong>Aflame</strong> rolls a Barrage die and takes damage accordingly.</li>
<li>If a player has <strong>no creatures or buildings</strong> on the board, the frontline advances <strong>two rows</strong> against them.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="main-phase">3. Main Phase</h3>
<p>Perform <strong>three actions</strong> in any combination from the following list:</p>
<h4 id="play-a-card">Play a Card</h4>
<p>Choose a card from your hand and place a corresponding marker on any valid square in your territory. Place the card at the front edge of the board.</p>
<ul>
<li>There are <strong>five creature markers</strong> and <strong>three building markers</strong> available to each player. A card cannot be played if no corresponding marker is available.</li>
<li>When a creature is played, place the appropriate number of health counters on its card immediately.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="move-a-creature">Move a Creature</h4>
<p>Select a creature and move it to any square permitted by its <strong>movement pattern</strong>. Creatures may move into enemy territory.</p>
<ul>
<li>Orientation matters. You may rotate a creature either before or after moving, but <strong>not both in the same action</strong>.</li>
<li>You may use a move action to <strong>rotate only</strong>, without moving any squares.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="attack">Attack</h4>
<p>Select a friendly creature to perform an attack. Choose one <strong>occupied square</strong> within its attack pattern. Roll the number of dice shown for that square from the creature’s dice pool. Apply damage and any other effects shown on the dice.</p>
<ul>
<li>If any asset reaches <strong>0 HP</strong>, remove its marker from the board and discard the card.</li>
<li>A creature may attack a token on their own square using one die. A creature cannot heal themselves.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="use-an-ability">Use an Ability</h4>
<p>If a creature has a <strong>power counter</strong>, it may use the special ability described on its card.</p>
<h3 id="draw">4. Draw</h3>
<p>Draw cards back up to a hand of <strong>six</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="cards">Cards</h2>
<h3 id="creature-cards">Creature Cards</h3>
<p>Creatures are the core of the game. They move, attack, and push the frontlines to win. They can also advance deep into enemy territory to destroy buildings and infrastructure.</p>
<p>Each creature card includes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Element Type</strong> — The creature’s elemental affiliation.</li>
<li><strong>Maximum Health</strong> — The number of health counters placed on the creature when it enters play. When all counters are removed, the creature dies. A creature may never have more health counters than its maximum health value.</li>
<li><strong>Attack Pattern</strong> — A grid showing which squares the creature can target, relative to its position and orientation. Numbers indicate how many dice from its pool it may use for that square. A creature may also attack a token on its own square for 1 die.</li>
<li><strong>Dice Pool</strong> — The colors and quantities of dice available for attacks.</li>
<li><strong>Movement Pattern</strong> — A grid showing which squares the creature can move to.</li>
<li><strong>Special Ability</strong> — An ability usable only when the creature has a power counter.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="building-cards">Building Cards</h3>
<p>Buildings power creature abilities and provide passive effects. They are represented on the board by building markers. Buildings can still deal retaliatory damage when attacked.</p>
<p>Each building card includes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Element Type</strong> — The building’s elemental affiliation. It can only power creatures of the same type.</li>
<li><strong>Maximum Health</strong> — Health counters placed on the building when constructed. When all are removed, the building is destroyed. A building may never have more health counters than its maximum health value.</li>
<li><strong>Passive Ability</strong> — Automatically triggered each turn the building is <strong>not</strong> used to generate power.</li>
</ul>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Placement rule:</strong> A “valid” square is a square within a building’s range, unoccupied by enemy creatures, and obeys normal square occupancy rules.</p>
</blockquote>
<h2 id="infrastructure-tokens">Infrastructure Tokens</h2>
<p>Tokens are assets represented by markers on the board. Unlike buildings and creatures, they have no corresponding card. They are generated by certain buildings or creature abilities.</p>
<ul>
<li>Tokens have <strong>1 HP</strong> and are destroyed immediately upon taking any damage.</li>
<li>Creatures may <strong>share a square</strong> with a token. If a square with both present is attacked, the creature absorbs all hits first. Any remaining hits after the creature is killed carry over to the token.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="square-occupancy">Square Occupancy</h2>
<ul>
<li>Each square holds <strong>one creature or building</strong> at a time.</li>
<li>A creature and an infrastructure token <strong>may</strong> coexist on a square.</li>
<li>A token and a building <strong>may not</strong> coexist on a square.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="the-frontlines">The Frontlines</h2>
<p>The board begins split evenly between both players. Players may only place creatures and buildings within their own territory.</p>
<p>During combat, the <strong>Advance (Green)</strong> die can push the frontline. When an advancement icon is rolled, the frontline moves one row toward the defending player. Any defending buildings on the affected row are <strong>immediately destroyed</strong>.</p>
<p>The game ends when a player loses all of their territory. The player who successfully conquered the board is the winner.</p>
<h2 id="status-effects">Status Effects</h2>
<p>Status effects are counters placed on creature or building cards representing buffs or debuffs. Status effects are gained through special creature abilities, buildings’ passive abilities, and interactions with infrastructure tokens.</p>
<ul>
<li>When a <strong>debuffed</strong> asset is healed, one point of healing is spent removing the debuff before restoring HP.</li>
<li>When a <strong>buffed</strong> asset is attacked, one hit is spent removing the buff before dealing damage.</li>
<li>Status effects <strong>cannot</strong> be applied to infrastructure tokens.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="elemental-types">Elemental Types</h2>
<p>Every asset belongs to one of four elemental types. Each element includes four creatures, two buildings, a unique elemental ability, an associated status effect, and an infrastructure token type.</p>
<hr>
<h3 id="bomb">Bomb</h3>
<p><strong>Elemental Ability:</strong> Bomb assets explode when reduced to 0 HP, or <strong>immediately</strong> when set Aflame. On explosion, roll a Barrage die against all assets in the surrounding 8 squares — including friendly assets.</p>
<p><strong>Status Effect — Aflame:</strong> The affected asset takes a Barrage die of damage each Passive Actions phase.</p>
<p><strong>Infrastructure Token — Bombs:</strong> Placed by the Explosives Factory within its range. Like all Bomb assets, bomb tokens explode when destroyed.</p>
<hr>
<h3 id="corn">Corn</h3>
<p><strong>Elemental Ability:</strong> While inside a corn field, creatures take one fewer hit than shown on the dice during combat. While inside a corn field, a corn creature may use an action to become <strong>Buttered Up</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Status Effect — Buttered Up:</strong> The creature may reroll any one die when attacking. The status effect is lost once used.</p>
<p><strong>Infrastructure Token — Corn Fields:</strong> Used for various Corn mechanics, including enabling the Buttered Up action.</p>
<hr>
<h3 id="goop">Goop</h3>
<p><strong>Elemental Ability:</strong> When a Goop creature deals retaliatory damage, the attacker gains the <strong>Slimy</strong> status effect. Additionally, Goop creatures leave a pool of ooze on each square they move away from. If no ooze tokens remain, any existing token may be removed and replaced. Goop creatures are not affected by ooze.</p>
<p><strong>Status Effect — Slimy:</strong> Slimy creatures cannot be powered by buildings. If already powered when Slimy is applied, they may still use their abilities. Slimy buildings cannot be used to power creatures.</p>
<p><strong>Infrastructure Token — Ooze:</strong> Moving out of a square containing ooze costs <strong>two actions</strong> instead of one.</p>
<hr>
<h3 id="storm">Storm</h3>
<p><strong>Elemental Ability:</strong> When a Storm creature deals damage to a creature as the attacker, it <strong>must</strong> move that creature one square in any direction, including diagonally. This does not affect buildings or tokens.</p>
<p><strong>Status Effect — Sodden:</strong> Sodden creatures roll one fewer die on all rolls. If a creature is Aflame when given Sodden, Aflame is immediately removed.</p>
<p><strong>Infrastructure Token — Storm Cells:</strong> Any non-Storm creature that occupies a square with a storm cell gains the <strong>Sodden</strong> status effect.</p>
